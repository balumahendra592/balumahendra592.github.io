How an engagement runs

Three stages, in order — you can stop after any one of them.

1

Audit

I review your current infrastructure, deployment flow and monitoring, and hand you a short report: risks, gaps and a recommended architecture.

2

Fix

A fixed-scope project to implement it — Dockerize the app, wire up CI/CD, or move the deploy onto Kubernetes. You get working infrastructure and documentation.

3

Support

An optional monthly retainer to keep it running: monitoring, troubleshooting, security checks and incident support as your team grows.
